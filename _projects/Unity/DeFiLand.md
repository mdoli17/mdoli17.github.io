## Overview

DeFi-Land is a framing-simulation project blending casual mini-games with blockchain-based progression.

My focus was on deseigning smooth, extensible gameplay loops and ensuring player interactions felt rewarding.

---

## Highlights

- Developed <b>core farming mini-games</b> (Fising, Shooting Range, Building/Decoration)
- Implemented <b>UI authentication, main HUD, and in-game menus</b>
- Built blockchain integrations: <b>wallet connection, token transactions, NFT management, Staking, Swaps and Yield Farming.
- Worked closely with designers/artists to align mechanics with visuals and user experience.
- Maintained <b>code architecture for scalability</b> as features expanded.
