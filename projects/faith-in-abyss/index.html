Stealth Game Prototype

Faith In Abyss

Overview

A first-person stealth prototype exploring advanced AI systems built with Unreal Engine 5. The project showcases my ability to extend existing frameworks, design systemic AI, and deliver responsive stealth gameplay.

Highlights

  • Built an extended AI Perception system with custom Safezone sense.
  • Combined finite state machines, Behavior Trees, and State Trees for layered decision-making.
  • Integrated pathfinding with dynamic investigation points projected on the navmesh.
  • Designed patrol, search, and chase behaviors reactive to sound and sight.
  • Developed AI debugging overlays for faster iteration.
