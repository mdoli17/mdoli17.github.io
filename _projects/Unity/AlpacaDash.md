## Overview

Alpaca Dash is a online racing game where players choose alpacas based on their stats and skills which they have unlocked to race on dynamic, obstacle-filled tracks.

I was responseible for core gameplay engineering, implementing systems that made the racing feel smooth, reactive, and replayable.

Available to play here: [Alpaca Dash Website](https://play.almightyalpacas.com/)

---

## Highlights

- Implemented <b>racer movement along Bezier tracks</b>, ensuring natural flow.
- Built the <b>race track mesh generation pipeline</b> with support for multiple lanes and UV modes.
- Designed the <b>procedural obstacle generation system</b> to keep races unpredictable and fun.
- Developed <b>responsive UI and VFX</b> for clear player feedback.
- Contributed <b>tools and backend integrations</b> to streamline team workflows.
